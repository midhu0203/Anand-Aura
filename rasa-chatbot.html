Why Rasa

Built on an open, flexible framework.

Rasa gives us full control over how your chatbot understands intent and holds a conversation — instead of being boxed in by a rigid, generic chatbot builder.

  • Custom intents & entities — trained on your business, your terms, your products.
  • Multi-turn dialogue — handles follow-up questions, not just one-off replies.
  • Fallback handling — hands off to a human cleanly when it should.
  • n8n-ready — every resolved conversation can trigger a downstream workflow.
